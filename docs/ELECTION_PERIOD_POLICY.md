# NetaMonitor Election-Period Safeguards

**Phase:** PH-00 · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** PROPOSED policy framework; legal gate unresolved
**Decision owner:** Human project owner · **Required review:** Qualified counsel and applicable election authorities/guidance

## 1. Purpose and limits

This is a risk-control framework, not legal advice or a universal safe harbour. NetaMonitor is not an election system. Citizen evaluations are not official ballots, an election poll, a mandate, proof of performance or endorsement. Applicable rules may vary by jurisdiction, election type, content, audience, timing and publication method; counsel must review current law and authoritative guidance before activation.

Q11 (jurisdiction-specific wording/restrictions) is open. No election-mode trigger, suppression period, legal interpretation, universal blackout or publication rule is approved in PH-00.

## 2. Risk triggers for human review

Potential triggers include a notified local/state/national election; relevant campaign or silence periods identified by counsel; election-related complaint or authority notice; uncertainty whether an aggregate resembles a poll/exit poll; political advertising/sponsorship request; coordinated manipulation; a disputed campaign claim; and a change in applicable law or authority guidance.

Triggers request review; they do not automatically assert illegality or activate a restriction absent an approved policy.

## 3. Proposed control framework

If the owner and counsel approve a jurisdiction-specific configuration, it should specify:

- Jurisdiction, election, covered office/constituency, effective start/end and authoritative source.
- Authorized proposer and independent approver; no single operator may silently change mode.
- Exact features/content affected: evaluation collection, public aggregates, leaderboard, notifications, promotion, new political content, imports or comments.
- User-facing neutral notice, scope, dates, reason category, contact/appeal route and approved translations.
- Treatment of existing factual records, corrections, right of reply and safety reports; do not silently delete or rewrite historical facts.
- Audit event, change notification, review cadence, expiry/rollback and incident handling.
- Legal review date, named counsel/owner and evidence of authority/guidance.

Default behavior when configuration is absent, expired, contradictory or unverifiable should be fail-safe and escalated to authorized review; exact operational fallback must be approved before deployment. No person should interpret this draft alone as permission to suppress lawful civic information.

## 4. Prohibited practices and neutral treatment

- No political advertising, paid ranking or paid vote weighting in MVP.
- No partisan symbols, selective application of restrictions, campaign promotion or targeted persuasion.
- No relabelling citizen evaluation as official poll/result.
- No suppression of a viewpoint merely because it is critical or favorable.
- No universal legal deadline/window invented by the platform.
- Keep editorial evidence, citizen opinion and data coverage separate during any pause.

## 5. Incident and escalation

Preserve the record and configuration history; route notices/complaints to named counsel and owner; pause only the specifically approved feature/scope if policy requires; log actors/reasons; communicate neutral status; and review impacts on accessibility, corrections, replies and non-election public access. Record external authority communications under restricted access and a retention decision.

## 6. Decision gates

Q11 wording and jurisdiction rules; legal applicability and election calendar source; trigger and approval authority; scope/duration of each restriction; appeal/notice process; public aggregates and notification behavior; incident contacts; and election-period testing are unresolved. PH-25 cannot activate a mode without approved policy, legal review and an audit-ready operating process.

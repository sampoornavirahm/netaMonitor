# NetaMonitor Content Governance and Moderation Policy

**Phase:** PH-00 · **Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Draft for owner/editorial/legal review

## 1. Purpose and separation

Moderation protects participants and service integrity; editorial review evaluates evidence and public factual status. Neither substitutes for the other. Citizen evaluations are opinion, not proof. Political viewpoint, party, criticism, praise or popularity alone is not a moderation ground. No paid placement/ranking or political advertising is permitted in MVP.

## 2. Source and claim taxonomy

Label material by origin and review, not by presumed truth:

- Official/primary record (identify issuing body and source/date; does not guarantee completeness).
- Credible secondary source (identify publisher, author/date and method where available).
- User-submitted source/material (clearly unreviewed until accepted).
- Unverified claim or disputed item (not presented as adjudicated fact).
- Editorial adjudication (status/reason/reviewer role/date and evidence links).

Every material public claim requires provenance, attribution, date/status, source-use review, uncertainty where material and a correction route. Preserve original language/context and label translations. Do not convert an association, allegation, protest attendance, business interest or financial disclosure into proof of wrongdoing.

## 3. Review and publication

Proposed workflow: submission/draft → safety and completeness triage → source/attribution review → sensitive-content escalation where needed → independent approval/adjudication → versioned publication or reasoned return/rejection. Author cannot approve own work. Review decisions include sources, rationale, confidence/status vocabulary, reviewer role, date and policy version. Sensitive claims (criminal conduct, health, identity, financial/business allegation, private contact or imminent harm) require restricted handling and qualified editorial/legal review; exact escalation standards need owner review.

Unreviewed submissions are labelled "User-submitted · not yet reviewed" and cannot alter score/status. A reply is displayed distinctly and never replaces an original source-backed record. Factual disputes use correction workflow, with prior versions retained and a different reviewer for appeal.

## 4. Moderation scope and action

Apply to comments, submissions, profile replies, media/uploads, user reports, impersonation, spam and threats. Proposed actions, proportionate to documented behavior: no action; label/context; request edit; limit distribution while reviewed; remove or restrict content; temporary posting restriction; account suspension; escalation to safety/legal/security. Do not prescribe law enforcement action or legal thresholds without qualified review.

Each action records the rule, evidence, scope, duration where relevant, reviewer, reason, notice and appeal. Emergency action may limit immediate harm but requires prompt independent review. Avoid exposing reporter identities or anti-abuse signals. Provide a reason and appeal route unless doing so creates a specific safety risk documented by an authorized reviewer.

## 5. User rights, corrections and appeal

- Offer report/correction routes from relevant public provenance cues.
- Give submitters a reference and status; do not promise a response time until operational capacity is defined.
- Publish accepted factual corrections as dated notices linked to the prior version.
- Treat right of reply as a distinct, moderated response; a claimant cannot suppress correct sourced facts.
- Assign appeals to a different reviewer; preserve reasons and outcomes.
- Allow an authorized reviewer to restrict personal data while correcting the public record without erasing audit history.

## 6. Neutrality, conflicts and staff

Apply the same source, evidence, moderation and disclosure standards regardless of political viewpoint or office. Require staff conflict-of-interest declaration for relevant content; recuse or independently reassign when conflicted. Keep editor, reviewer, moderator, verifier, support and analytics permissions separated. No self-approval. Publish policy/methodology changes with version and effective date.

## 7. Financial, affiliation and sensitive content

Financial facts state period, scope, currency/unit, source and basis: self-declared, officially recorded or independently corroborated. Do not call disclosed assets "net worth" without a justified methodology and evidence. Affiliations use exact relationship/role and dates; participation is not membership. Private data, unverified allegations, doxxing, threats and personal identity evidence are not made public merely because they were submitted.

## 8. Operational and legal questions

Comment/reaction scope (Q02), profile-claim authority (Q10), reviewer bylines (Q09), notice/appeal timing, evidence-confidence language, safety escalation, conflict disclosure and election-period handling (Q11) remain open. Obtain qualified legal review for defamation, privacy, user-generated content, election and notice obligations. This document does not determine legal compliance.
